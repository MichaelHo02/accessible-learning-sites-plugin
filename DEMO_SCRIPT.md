# Three-minute demo script

Target: **2:45–2:55**. Keep every screen ready before recording and speak as if
you are showing the project to another teacher.

## 1. Start with the teacher's situation — 0:00–0:20

**Show:** The Week 2 Kinematics PDF, recording, and transcript.

**Say:**

> Let's imagine you're teaching a kinematics class tomorrow. You already have a
> PDF, a 90-minute recording, and a transcript. Some of your students have
> accessibility needs, but adapting everything by hand takes time. That's where
> the Accessible Learning Sites plugin comes in.

## 2. Show the plugin workflow — 0:20–0:45

**Show:** Accessible Learning Sites in ChatGPT Work, the selected Week 2 lesson,
Codex running the plugin, and the completed Site link. Cut out all waiting.

**Say:**

> I open ChatGPT Work, choose the plugin, and give it this one lesson. GPT-5.6
> Sol reads the documents, equations, diagrams, images, and transcript. Codex
> follows the plugin's workflow to rebuild the material, run structural checks,
> and create the Site. The plugin keeps the original order, objective, and
> difficulty. For a complex lesson, specialist agents can divide the source
> analysis, building, and checking. You still review everything before it is
> shared.

## 3. Showcase the accessibility controller — 0:45–1:30

**Show:** Open **Accessibility settings**. Switch to Vietnamese and back to
English. Set text to 150%, briefly open the five colour options, choose one,
switch to section-by-section reading, hide optional extras, then reset.

**Say:**

> Now look at what students can control. The whole lesson can switch between
> English and Vietnamese. Under Reading, they can change the text size, spacing,
> and width. Under Colour support, they can keep the lesson palette, increase
> differences, use minimal colour, or make red-green and blue-yellow pairs
> easier to distinguish. Under Attention, they can move through one section at
> a time and hide optional extras. Required content stays available, and Reset
> returns to the defaults. These choices stay on the device and are available
> to everyone without creating a student profile.

## 4. Show how the lesson was rebuilt — 1:30–2:25

**Show:** Open one timestamped transcript beside its lesson topic. Show MathML,
the problem-solving checklist, and one step-by-step explanation. In the
constant-acceleration simulator, select **Car**, change acceleration from 2 to
3, and point to the updated equation, visual, result, and data table.

**Say:**

> Now look at the lesson itself. This is not the PDF embedded in a webpage.
> Each transcript keeps the timestamps, speaker, and unclear words, and can play
> the recording from that exact moment. Equations use MathML. Key points, plain
> explanations, a checklist, and step-by-step support make the reasoning easier
> to follow without changing the academic task. In this simulator, I can use a
> point or a car and change the acceleration. The equation, visual, text result,
> and data table all update together, so the idea is not trapped in animation or
> colour alone.

## 5. Close with the platform story — 2:25–2:55

**Show:** Briefly show the plugin's skill, Sites connector, specialist agents,
and starter prompts or reusable templates. Finish on the published Site and URL.

**Say:**

> The OpenAI platform is not just how we built this; it is part of the product.
> The plugin brings together an accessibility skill, the Sites connector,
> specialist agents, starter prompts, and reusable lesson templates. GPT-5.6
> understands the source, Codex rebuilds and checks the lesson, and Sites lets
> you review, publish, share, and update it. You can also schedule a daily or
> weekly folder watch that turns one new lesson into an owner-only draft for
> review, while sharing stays under your control. The result targets WCAG 2.2 AA
> and gives the whole class more ways to access the same learning.

## Recording checklist

- Keep the final video below three minutes; aim for 2:55.
- Say **Codex** and **GPT-5.6** aloud.
- Show the controller and one simulator clearly instead of rushing through
  every feature.
- Remove loading, installation, and long typing from the final edit.
- Add accurate captions and keep text and cursor movement easy to follow.
- Say **targets WCAG 2.2 AA**.
- Test the public or unlisted YouTube link while signed out.
